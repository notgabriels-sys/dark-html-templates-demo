Questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes, at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately and downgrades at the end of the period. Everything is prorated to the day.

What happens if I exceed my storage?

Nothing breaks. We email you and you have 14 days to upgrade or clear space before writes are paused.

Do you offer refunds?

Full refund within 30 days, no questions asked. Reply to any invoice and we'll process it same day.

Is there a discount for non-profits?

Yes — 50% off any plan. Email us from your organisation's domain and we'll apply it.
